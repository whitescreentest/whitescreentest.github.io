About Us

WhiteScreen is a free web tool with one job: fill your screen with a single solid color, so you can see exactly what your display is doing.

The idea is simple. A screen hides its faults behind whatever you are looking at — a photo, a video, a web page. Fill it with pure white and a dead pixel becomes a dark dot. Fill it with black and backlight bleed shows up at the edges. Fill it with red, green or blue and you can tell which subpixel is stuck. No app can test a screen better than a plain color can, and no test is faster than opening a page and pressing a button.

That is why we built this tool for the browser. Open the page, pick a color, go full screen — done. It works on phones, tablets, laptops, monitors and smart TVs, because every one of them has a browser.

What the tool does

  • Solid color screens — white, black, red, green, blue, yellow, orange, pink and grey, plus any custom color you pick.
  • Full screen mode — one click fills the whole display, so nothing distracts from the test.
  • Zoom Light — a warm, soft white that lights your face for video calls.
  • Ring Light — a glowing white ring on black, a quick stand-in for a ring light.
  • Download — save any color as a PNG at 720p, 1080p, 4K or a custom size.

Who we are

We also run the SpeakerCleaners tool. WhiteScreen is our second free tool — same idea, different problem: instead of cleaning a speaker with sound, it checks a screen with color.

Our promise

The tool is free and will stay free. There are no accounts, no subscriptions, no locked colors and no downloads. We do not collect your data — the color is drawn on your own device, in your browser, and nothing leaves it.

If the tool helped you spot a fault — or lit your face on a call — share it with someone who just bought a new screen.
